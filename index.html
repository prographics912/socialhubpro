Questions

Good to know before we start.

Can I request just one service?

Yes. You can ask for an individual marketing, website, IT/AI, or sourcing project. We’ll confirm whether it fits our current capabilities and provide a separate scope and quote.

What does the $100 Trial Pack include?

It is a one-time 30-day offer for one customer, combining a focused marketing starter scope with one eligible digital-service review or consultation. The exact additional service is agreed before payment.

Are ad spend and software costs included?

No. Advertising budgets, subscriptions, paid tools, and third-party fees are separate unless explicitly included in a written proposal.

Do you guarantee results?

No specific reach, revenue, ranking, or automation outcome can be guaranteed. We agree on the work, track relevant measures where possible, and improve based on evidence.
